How to draw... a curled feather

Use one curved quill as the backbone, add two unequal feather vanes, then break the right edge and shade the form. This 20-minute pencil sketch practices long curves and directional graphite.

20 minEasy

Pencil ready?

Let's draw a curled feather

Treat this as one small practice round: build the subject lightly, notice the shapes, then darken only the lines that help the finished sketch.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperWhatever you already have
  • HB pencilWhatever you already have
  • 2B pencilWhatever you already have
  • Kneaded eraserCompletely optional

No colored pencil? Graphite alone makes a complete sketch.

  1. 01

    Set the quill

    Start low on the left and draw a gently bowed line toward an upper-right tip; thicken only its lower end into a short quill.

    Sketch tip: Ghost the rising curve before touching down; keep the lower quill short.

  2. 02

    Sweep the left vane

    From the quill base, sweep one long convex edge left of the shaft and bring it into the existing upper tip.

    Sketch tip: Use the same upper tip, and leave open paper to the right for the second vane.

  3. 03

    Close the right vane

    From that same upper tip, curve a narrower edge down the right side and return it to the quill base without moving the shaft.

    Sketch tip: Keep the right vane narrower so the shaft remains off center.

  4. 04

    Break the vane edge

    Leave three tiny gaps along the right edge and pull a few short slanted barbs from those gaps toward the shaft.

    Sketch tip: Count three edge breaks and aim each short barb toward the central shaft.

  5. 05

    Ground the feather

    Lay a few short horizontal graphite strokes beneath the lower quill and left vane, leaving white paper between them.

    Sketch tip: Leave gaps between shadow strokes so the feather still feels light.

  6. 06

    Shade the feather

    Use light graphite strokes from the shaft toward both edges, darken the quill and broken ground shadow, and leave narrow paper highlights.

    Sketch tip: Shade out from the shaft rather than along the length of the feather.
