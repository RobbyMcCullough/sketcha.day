How to draw... a wooden spoon

Build the spoon from a tilted bowl and a long connected handle, then show its hollow rim, carved grain, and soft shadow. This 20-minute sketch practices a simple diagonal silhouette and restrained wood texture.

20 minEasy

Pencil ready?

Let's draw a wooden spoon

Treat this as one small practice round: build the subject lightly, notice the shapes, then darken only the lines that help the finished sketch.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperWhatever you already have
  • HB pencilWhatever you already have
  • 2B pencilWhatever you already have
  • Warm brown colored pencilCompletely optional

No colored pencil? Graphite alone makes a complete sketch.

  1. 01

    Outline the spoon bowl

    Draw a wide tilted oval bowl at the upper left, leaving a narrow gap at its lower-right edge for the handle.

    Sketch tip: Tilt the bowl before drawing; leave its lower-right gap open for the handle.

  2. 02

    Pull the long handle

    From the two open bowl ends, draw two gently converging lines toward the lower right and round their tip.

    Sketch tip: Let both handle edges start from the bowl gap and taper gently toward the tip.

  3. 03

    Carve the inner rim

    Inside the bowl, echo its oval with a shallow inner curve, keeping the rim narrowest where the handle joins.

    Sketch tip: Keep the inner curve close to the outer bowl edge to show thickness without making a second spoon.

  4. 04

    Mark the wood grain

    Add three short curved grain lines along the handle and two small crescent marks inside the bowl.

    Sketch tip: Use three short handle curves and two small bowl crescents; follow the wood's direction.

  5. 05

    Ground the spoon

    Put a broken cast shadow just below the bowl and handle, leaving white paper between shadow and outer contour.

    Sketch tip: Break the shadow into soft strokes and leave a narrow paper gap from the spoon.

  6. 06

    Shade the carved wood

    Layer warm brown pencil along the established bowl and handle, leaving a pale highlight in the bowl; deepen the existing ground shadow with soft graphite.

    Sketch tip: Shade with the side of the pencil and preserve a pale highlight in the bowl.
