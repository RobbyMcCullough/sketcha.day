How to draw... a garden trowel

Build the scoop first, attach its narrow neck and wooden grip, then place a little soil at the tip. This 20-minute pencil sketch practices a diagonal tool silhouette and restrained graphite texture.

20 minEasy
Start drawing

Pencil ready?

Let's draw a garden trowel

Treat this as one small practice round: build the subject lightly, notice the shapes, then darken only the lines that help the finished sketch.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperWhatever you already have
  • HB pencilWhatever you already have
  • 2B pencilWhatever you already have
  • Kneaded eraserCompletely optional

No colored pencil? Graphite alone makes a complete sketch.

  1. 01

    Shape the scoop

    Draw a pointed scoop as two gently curved sides meeting at a rounded tip.

    Sketch tip: Leave both upper scoop shoulders open for the neck.

  2. 02

    Attach the neck

    From the broad top of the blade, draw a narrow neck that meets both blade shoulders.

    Sketch tip: Connect the neck to both scoop shoulders.

  3. 03

    Draw the grip

    From the narrow neck, add a small collar and a rounded wooden grip angled up-right; close its top.

    Sketch tip: Keep the grip on the same diagonal axis.

  4. 04

    Set the soil

    Under the blade tip, sketch a low irregular mound with a clear contact point.

    Sketch tip: Let the mound touch only the tip.

  5. 05

    Add small details

    Add three small soil crumbs beside the mound and two short grain marks inside the handle.

    Sketch tip: Count three crumbs and two grip marks.

  6. 06

    Shade the trowel

    Darken the keeper lines, shade one side of the blade and grip, and hatch the soil mound lightly.

    Sketch tip: Let the paper tooth show through the shading.
